# Premium Website Design Brief — Terraforge Engineering

Design a **premium, modern, high-end corporate website** for a construction and engineering company called **Terraforge Engineering**.

Terraforge Engineering specializes in **construction, engineering, interior design, renovation, and related building solutions**. The website should communicate **strength, precision, craftsmanship, reliability, professionalism, and modern architecture**.

The overall design should feel like a premium construction/engineering brand — sophisticated and architectural rather than a generic construction template.

---

## 1. Overall Design Direction

Create a visually impressive, premium website inspired by modern architecture, engineering, luxury real estate, and interior design websites.

### Design Style

* Premium
* Modern
* Minimal but visually rich
* Architectural
* Professional
* Strong typography
* High-quality photography
* Clean layouts
* Generous whitespace
* Strong visual hierarchy
* Subtle animations and micro-interactions
* Elegant image transitions
* Sophisticated cards and sections
* Responsive across desktop, tablet, and mobile

Avoid:

* Generic construction website layouts
* Overuse of gradients
* Excessive rounded cards
* Cartoon-style icons
* Cluttered layouts
* Too many colors
* Cheap-looking stock-template aesthetics

The website should look like it was designed by a **professional architectural/branding agency**.

* Strong
* Precise
* Reliable

* Experienced

### Suggested Color Palette

Use an architectural and earthy color palette:

* Deep charcoal / near-black
* Warm off-white
* Stone / concrete gray
* Sand / beige
* Muted bronze or copper as an accent

Use the accent color sparingly for buttons, highlights, active states, and important UI elements.

The design should have a strong contrast between dark architectural sections and clean light sections.
